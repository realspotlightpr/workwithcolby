# Free SEO agent prompt library

## 1. Technical audit

> Audit every indexable page in this repository. Build an evidence table for status, title, description, H1, canonical, schema, internal links, images, sitemap inclusion and mobile risks. Separate observed problems from recommendations. Rank fixes by impact, effort and confidence. Make no external changes.

## 2. Keyword-to-page map

> Build a keyword and search-intent map for the existing pages. Assign one primary intent to one primary page, identify cannibalization, and propose missing pages only where the site can provide a genuinely distinct answer. Do not create thin pages.

## 3. Content brief

> Create a content brief for [TOPIC]. Include reader intent, primary question, unique angle, outline, required evidence, internal links, conversion CTA, claims that need verification and topics to avoid duplicating.

## 4. Internal-link audit

> Find pages with weak or missing internal links. Recommend contextual source paragraph, destination page and natural anchor language. Do not repeat exact-match anchors mechanically.

## 5. Backlink opportunity research

> Research legitimate profile, citation, partner, resource and editorial opportunities relevant to this business. For each, record eligibility, cost, submission URL, relationship fit, likely link attributes when known and required owner action. Reject paid-link schemes and mass directories. Do not submit or contact anyone.

## 6. Outreach draft

> Draft one personalized outreach message for [TARGET]. Explain why the site or resource is genuinely relevant, what value we can contribute and the most appropriate destination URL. Do not send it. Avoid generic compliments and reciprocal-link offers.

## 7. Monthly review

> Compare this month with the previous period. Separate seasonality and measurement changes from likely SEO movement. Summarize wins, losses, anomalies, completed work, unresolved risks and the next three evidence-backed priorities. Never claim causation from correlation alone.
